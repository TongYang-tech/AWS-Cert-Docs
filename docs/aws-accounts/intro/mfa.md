# Mult-Factor Auth (MFA)

### Why MFA is Needed?

You typically log in to any app using a username & password. What happens if those creds are compromised? Then anyone can impersonate you and gain unauth access. MFA helps reduce this risk by adding extra layers of security.

### What is a Factor?

A factor is a piece of evidence to **identify who you are**. There are different types of factors including:

- Single-factor auth: Using one type of factor (username & password)
- Mult-factor auth: Using multiple factors, making it harder for an unauthorized party to impersonate you

### Key Takeaways

- MFA combines multiple factors to verify identity, enhancing security.
- AWS supports both physical MFA devices and virtual MFA apps (e.g., Google Authenticator).
- Configuring MFA protects your AWS identity by requiring both something you know and something you have.
